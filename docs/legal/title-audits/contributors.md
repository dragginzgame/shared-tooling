evidence and not a conclusion about ownership.

does not reproduce contributor email addresses, signatures or agreements. Exact
Git identity variants and supporting documents belong in the private legal
evidence system. A matched identity answers *who appears to have committed*; it
does not establish that Toko Studios Limited owns or may relicense the work.

## Agreement priorities

1. Put current unsigned contractors and contributors under signed terms before
   accepting more contributions. The agreement should cover both future work and
   an attached schedule of historical repositories, commits or date ranges.
2. Seek retrospective assignments or CLAs from former unsigned contractors while
   they remain contactable and cooperative.
3. Review the Mana Point agreements and document the complete transfer or licence
   chain into Toko Studios Limited. An agreement with another company is not, by
   itself, evidence that Toko Studios Limited owns the rights.
4. Perform commit-level review for uncertain or apparently minimal contributors.
   Preserve the decision and immutable commit references in the relevant
   repository record.
5. Record signed-document digests, custodians and opaque evidence identifiers in
   the private evidence system, then update each affected repository audit. Do
   not commit the signed documents or personal identity evidence here.

## Clearance rule

An affected repository remains amber or red until an authorised reviewer has
linked every retained material contribution to verified employment scope, an
effective assignment, the signed Toko Studios CLA, or an independently
applicable licence. Identity reconciliation alone never changes a repository to
green.
