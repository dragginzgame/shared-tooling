# Toko Studios software title-audit register

**Audit date:** 10 November 2026

**Status:** Preliminary evidence inventory; not a legal conclusion of ownership.

This register covers the 59 repositories returned for the `dragginzgame` GitHub organisation and four additional local checkouts whose remotes were not present in that organisation listing. Repository membership, Git authorship and a licence notice are evidence inputs, not proof of title.

Detailed findings are kept one file per repository so each title chain can be reviewed, signed and updated independently. The register is the portfolio-level view and must remain consistent with those records.

The [contributor identity and rights register](contributors.md) normalises the
Git aliases identified by the maintainer and records the present agreement gaps.
It is a routing aid for the repository audits, not proof of ownership.

## Status meanings

- **Red — discrepancy:** a material licence, repository or title-record issue is already visible.
- **Amber — evidence incomplete:** no blocking discrepancy was established in the first pass, but signed ownership evidence has not been verified.
- **Unreviewed:** GitHub metadata is recorded but no local source or history review was possible.

No repository is green until authorised legal review verifies every material contributor, employer or contractor relationship, assignment or CLA, third-party component and applicable patent record.

## Portfolio-level findings

1. Signed assignments and accepted contributor agreements were not available in the repositories, so no repository has a verified chain of title.
2. The local checkouts for `ic-build`, `ic-delegated-auth`, `ic-memory` and `ic-testkit` were absent from the current organisation listing. Local migration evidence identifies `ic-build` and `ic-testkit` as consolidated into `ic-tooling`, and identifies `ic-memory` history and packages as consolidated with Storage. The exact remote retirement and successor chain still requires verification. `ic-delegated-auth` has no equivalent verified disposition record in this pass.
3. Thirty-seven inspected repositories lack a root licence or notice: six local
   checkouts and thirty-one remote-only roots. They are identified as red in
   their records. Absence of a licence does not prove Company ownership and can
   leave package or distribution terms unclear.
4. Apache or GNU metadata remains in `ic-platform`, `ic-storage`, `ic-tooling` and `toko`. Those entries may reflect third-party or previously granted rights and must be traced rather than overwritten.
5. `ic-relay` and `ic-wire` use an earlier Dragginz burn licence, while `icydb` uses the prior Toko Studios burn licence. Existing grants cannot be erased by adopting TSNL-1.0 prospectively.
6. Maintainer-supplied mappings now reconcile the observed display aliases for
   Adam, Donna Powell, Don Adriano, Gabriel, Remco, Selami, Phil Harvey, Emanuel
   Caravan, Joey Spencer and the other named individuals in the
   [contributor register](contributors.md). These mappings identify people but
   do not clear rights. Several current and former contractors have no recorded
   agreement, and the reported Mana Point employee agreements still require
   scope review and a verified chain into Toko Studios Limited.
7. Maintainer-supplied portfolio history identifies `shared-tooling` as the predecessor of `toko-tooling` and identifies `ic-scheduling` as a combination including `ic-timers`. The predecessor histories and rights must be included in the successor audits even if the old repositories are later removed.
8. One remote-only repository, `toko-miner-web`, reports size zero and did not
   return a root listing. Its title record remains unreviewed rather than being
   treated as a missing-licence finding.

## Completion rule

A repository becomes green only after counsel or an authorised title reviewer records the exact reviewed commit, confirms the legal owner of every material first-party contribution, classifies every third-party component and prior licence, verifies patent and moral-rights treatment, and links the signed private records without publishing personal documents.
