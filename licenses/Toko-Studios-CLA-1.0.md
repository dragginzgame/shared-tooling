## 2. Definitions and scope

**Contribution** means original source code, object code, documentation,
designs, tests, data structures, schemas, specifications or other copyrightable
or patentable material intentionally submitted by or for the Contributor for
inclusion in a Covered Project through the accounts, personnel and submission
routes identified in the Acceptance Record.

**Covered Project** means each repository or other project expressly identified
in the Acceptance Record. A renamed or transferred repository remains covered
where its history establishes continuity with an identified Covered Project.

Contributions submitted after the effective date are covered. Earlier
contributions are covered only where the Acceptance Record or an attached
schedule identifies them by repository and commit, pull request, date range or
another objectively determinable description.

Material clearly marked in writing as not being a contribution before its
submission is excluded. Third-party material is governed by Section 7.

## 3. Copyright assignment

In consideration of the Company's evaluation or acceptance of Contributions,
the mutual promises in this agreement and other good and valuable consideration,
the Contributor assigns to the Company, with full title guarantee to the extent
permitted by law, all worldwide right, title and interest in copyright and
related economic rights in each Contribution, including:

a. rights to reproduce, modify, adapt, translate, arrange and create derivative
works;

b. rights to compile, perform, display, communicate, make available, distribute,
publish, sell, license and otherwise exploit Contributions;

c. rights in source, object, native, WebAssembly, generated and other forms;

d. rights to combine Contributions with proprietary or third-party material;

e. rights to use Contributions on any blockchain, network, private system or
other infrastructure;

f. rights to grant licences and sublicences through any number of tiers under
open-source, source-available, proprietary, commercial or other terms; and

g. the right to enforce the assigned rights and, to the extent legally
assignable, recover for infringement occurring after the effective date.

The assignment is perpetual and applies for the full term of each right,
including renewals, revivals, extensions and rights arising under later law.

## 4. Future rights and further assurance

To the extent a Contribution or relevant right does not exist at signing, the
Contributor assigns it prospectively to the Company immediately when it comes
into existence. Where applicable law does not permit a present assignment of a
future right, the Contributor must hold that right for the Company and assign
it promptly after it arises.

The Contributor must execute reasonable documents and provide reasonable
assistance necessary to confirm, perfect, register or enforce the rights granted
by this agreement. The Company will reimburse reasonable pre-approved expenses
for assistance requested after submission.

## 5. Protective fallback licence

If any assignment in Sections 3 or 4 is invalid, ineffective or unenforceable,
the Contributor grants the Company a perpetual, worldwide, irrevocable,
non-exclusive, fully paid-up, royalty-free, transferable and sublicensable
licence to exercise every right described in Section 3.

This fallback licence is intended to survive termination, withdrawal,
assignment of the underlying rights, a change in control, insolvency and the
Contributor's death or dissolution to the maximum extent permitted by law.
The Contributor must not transfer or encumber a right without preserving this
licence and giving the transferee written notice of it.

## 6. Moral rights and personal rights

To the maximum extent permitted by law, the Contributor irrevocably waives and
agrees not to assert moral rights, droit moral and similar personal rights in a
Contribution against the Company or its licensees, sublicensees, successors and
assigns.

Where waiver is not permitted, the Contributor gives irrevocable consent to the
acts described in Section 3, including modification, combination, omission,
publication without attribution and publication under a project or company
name. This Section does not permit false personal attribution where applicable
law prohibits it.

## 7. Authority and third-party material

The Contributor represents, to the best of its knowledge after reasonable
inquiry, that:

a. it has authority to enter this agreement and grant the stated rights;

b. each Contribution is original to the Contributor except for material
identified in writing before acceptance;

c. no Contribution is subject to an employment, contractor, funding,
confidentiality, security, export, government or other obligation inconsistent
with this agreement;

d. it has disclosed all known co-authors, employers, clients and other persons
who may own or control relevant rights;

e. it has disclosed third-party code, data, generated output, model output and
other material together with its source and applicable terms; and

f. it has not knowingly granted or promised conflicting exclusive rights or
security interests.

Third-party material is excluded from the assignment and licence except to the
extent the Contributor actually controls and expressly identifies the rights it
grants. The Company must review independently applicable terms before inclusion.
Repository presence, dependency metadata or a Contributor representation does
not by itself establish Company ownership.

The Contributor must promptly disclose a material error in its ownership,
authority or third-party-material information.

## 8. Patent licence and non-assertion

The Contributor grants the Company and its affiliates, successors, assigns,
licensees and sublicensees a perpetual, worldwide, irrevocable, non-exclusive,
fully paid-up, royalty-free, transferable and sublicensable patent licence under
claims the Contributor now or later owns or controls that would be infringed by:

a. a Contribution alone;

b. a Contribution combined with a Covered Project or another Contribution;

c. modification, replacement, migration or continued development of those
materials; or

d. the Company's or its recipients' manufacture, use, operation, hosting,
distribution, licensing, sale, import or commercialisation of those materials
on any infrastructure.

The licence includes the right to make, have made, use, offer, sell, import,
export and otherwise exploit the covered subject matter. It does not cover a
claim infringed only by unrelated material independently added by the Company.

The Contributor covenants not to initiate, direct, finance or assist a claim
inconsistent with this Section. A transfer of a covered patent must preserve
this licence and covenant to the maximum extent permitted by law.

## 9. Company rights and Contributor use

The Company may use, keep private, publish, stop publishing, relicense, sell,
assign or decline a Contribution without further permission or payment. It has
no obligation to accept, review, maintain or exploit a Contribution.

Unless an Acceptance Record states otherwise, the Company grants the
Contributor a non-exclusive licence to use the Contributor's own Contribution
for personal and internal purposes. That licence does not include confidential
Company material, other contributors' material, Company trademarks or a right
to distribute the combined Covered Project contrary to its applicable licence.

## 10. Confidentiality, trademarks and personal data

This agreement does not create a confidentiality obligation or authorise the
Contributor to submit secrets or personal data. Confidential submissions require
a separate written agreement.

No trademark, trade name, domain, social account or endorsement right is granted
to the Contributor. The Company may use the Contributor's identified account
name and attribution information to administer contribution and ownership
records, subject to applicable data-protection law.

## 11. Duration, survival and complete agreement

The Contributor may stop making future Contributions. That does not withdraw or
terminate rights already assigned or licensed. Sections 3 through 10 survive
termination to the maximum extent permitted by law.

This agreement and its signed Acceptance Record are the complete agreement about
their subject matter. A repository file, later licence version, pull request or
informal statement does not amend them. An amendment requires a written agreement
signed by both parties.

If a provision is invalid or unenforceable, it must be narrowed only as necessary
and the remaining provisions continue. A failure or delay to enforce is not a
waiver.
